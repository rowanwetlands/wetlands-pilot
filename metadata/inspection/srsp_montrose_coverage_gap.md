# SRSP Montrose Basin coverage assessment

Assessment date: 2026-07-17

## Finding

No SRSP LiDAR dataset identified in the portal provides coverage of the
Montrose Basin SSSI core.

One inspected survey contains two tiles along the western edge, but these do
not cover the basin and are not suitable as the primary Phase 1 terrain source.

## Evidence status

STRUCTURAL_GAP

## Consequences

- Do not reduce the acquisition AOI to match available coverage.
- Do not combine unrelated survey epochs to manufacture full coverage.
- Do not interpolate missing basin terrain.
- Retain western-edge surveys only as possible secondary or historic evidence.
- Pursue alternative terrain sources using the agreed priority order.
